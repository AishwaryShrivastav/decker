# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We're building Decker to be a useful, open, and welcoming project. Everyone who participates — whether opening an issue, submitting a PR, or asking a question — deserves to be treated with respect.

## Standards

**Be constructive:**
- Assume good intentions
- Give feedback on the code, not the person
- Welcome newcomers and first-time contributors

**Be direct:**
- Say what you mean
- Disagree openly and respectfully
- Move on when a decision is made

**Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Spam or off-topic promotion
- Publishing others' private information

## Scope

This applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to **aish@techforgood.studio**. Reports will be reviewed and acted on as appropriate, up to and including blocking from the project.
